I've seen SML twice now and listened to [a few](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kfbF6SD4LEk&t=565s&pp=ygUDc21s) [other performances](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1QV6wwsY3DM) online. Their first two albums blended chopped up snippets of their live shows with overdubs, blurring the lines between the original performance and what's been added after the fact. To contrast that with a long set of only 2 "songs" showcases another side of the group, while also showing a little bit how the previous sausages were made. Only the first song "Roundabouts" has been released as a near 25 minute "single", but I anticipate this being a favorite album of the year, much like last year's [_How You Been_](https://intlanthem.bandcamp.com/album/how-you-been). There's a simulataneous patience and urgency to the performance, building up a groove from a lone sequencer into a rippling current that ebbs and flows the whole time.
